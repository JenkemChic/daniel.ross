PROJECT 03

Union Cemetery
Visualization

Overview

A historical visualization that connects more than 78,000 cemetery records to mapped sections and uses time to show how Union Cemetery developed from its earliest burials to the present.

RoleData / UX / Development
PlatformWeb / Blender
StackPython / CSV / SVG / Blender
StatusIn Progress

Problem

Records lack spatial context

Burial records exist as text, dates, sections, and lots. On their own, they do not show where growth occurred or how the cemetery changed over time.

Response

Connect records to place

The project cleans and normalizes the records, matches them to historic section maps, and converts chronology into a spatial visualization.

Workflow

Four linked steps

The data pipeline moves from cemetery records to mapped placement and then to a time-based view of cemetery growth.

Clean Match Place Animate

02 / Prototype

Cemetery growth timeline

The prototype uses mapped grave markers and a year-based reveal to show the cemetery filling over time. Current work focuses on lot-level placement and extending the visualization across the full cemetery.
