PROJECT 01

Overview

A mobile-first gallery auction platform connecting physical exhibitions to QR entry, artwork discovery, bidding, and continued participation after patrons leave the venue.

RoleProduct / UX/UI / Development
PlatformMobile Web App
StackDjango / HTML / CSS / JavaScript
StatusWorking Prototype

Problem

Bidding stops at the venue

Physical exhibitions can separate viewing from participation once patrons leave the room, limiting the auction to a single place and moment.

Response

Move the auction to the phone

A QR entry point connects patrons to artwork, current bids, and the bidding flow from the device already in their hands.

Workflow

Four linked steps

The same mobile flow connects the physical exhibition to continued participation after a patron leaves the venue.

Scan Browse Bid Continue
